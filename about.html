What is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is automated software used by enterprise recruiters and hiring teams to ingest, parse, index, and filter incoming job applications. Most ATS parsers extract candidate data into plain text fields (Contact Details, Skills, Experience, Education) before ranking candidates against job description requirements.

Overly complex multi-column tables, decorative graphical elements, non-standard section titles, and images can cause ATS parsers to fail. ResumeCraft AI ensures your resume follows standard, machine-readable hierarchies that parse flawlessly.

The 100-Point Transparent Scoring System

1. Contact Information 10 pts

Evaluates completeness of full name, valid email structure, phone number, location, and verified LinkedIn/GitHub links.

2. Professional Summary 10 pts

Checks for concise 25–90 word summary mentioning target role and primary domain strengths.

3. Technical & Soft Skills 15 pts

Analyzes presence of programming languages, frameworks, developer tools, databases, and key soft competencies.

4. Work Experience / Internships 15 pts

Rewards strong past-tense action verbs (Architected, Developed, Optimized) and measurable metrics.

5. Projects Section 15 pts

Evaluates project titles, technology stacks, problem descriptions, outcomes, and code repository links.

6. Education Background 10 pts

Checks degree name, university/institution, graduation year, and academic coursework/grades.

7. Keywords & Job Alignment 10 pts

Extracts keywords from your target Job Description and calculates exact overlap vs missing keyword gaps.

8. ATS Formatting & Readability 10 pts

Ensures standard section headers, absence of wall-of-text blocks, and zero non-standard symbols.

9. Additional Value Sections 5 pts

Rewards verified certifications, hackathons, academic honors, and languages.

🔒 100% Local-First & Zero Cloud API Dependency

  • All resume drafts and scans are stored securely in local SQLite (database.db).
